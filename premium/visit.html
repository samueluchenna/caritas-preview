Questions

Before you ask

Can I see the hall before I decide?

Yes. Book a visit and see it at the time of day your event will be.

Is there parking?

Yes, on the compound, with nearby space for larger events. Number of spaces to confirm

Are the halls air-conditioned?

Guests describe the halls as air-conditioned. We confirm the set-up for your hall and your date. To confirm

How do I hold a date?

You see the hall first, then a deposit holds the date. The amount and the rules are shown before you pay. Rules to confirm

Can two events happen on the same day?

Yes. The halls are separate, so up to three events can run at the same time.
